Tournament Managers are QView users that most notably have the ability to create new Tournaments and who also can manage and act as full owners of the Tournaments they create.

To apply to become a Tournament Manager, you first need to have a QView account. If you don’t have a QView account yet, you can create an account here.

If you have a QView account, log in on the login page.

Once logged in, you will be taken to your user’s profile.

In your profile, in the sidebar menu click “Managed Tournaments”. If you are not currently a Tournament Manager, you will see the following:

In this case, to apply to be a Tournament Manager, share a little bit about yourself, if you are a district or field director, what Tournaments you direct or want to direct and where (geographically) - all of these are good information to share with the QView website administrators.

With this information added, click the button to submit it for review.

Once approved, when you navigate to this section of your profile you will see a table of Tournaments (or lack thereof initially) that you are the owner/creator/manager of.
