After becoming approved to create and manage your own Tournaments and after creating a Tournament and its Rooms, you are ready to set up your Divisions and Registration.

Divisions can fundamentally be considered groups of Teams that compete with each other. In this way, Divisions are uniquely related to the Registration process since Teams are registered by their coaches/leaders and to register a Team must be designated to compete under a specific Division.

Creating Divisions

Let’s create a Division. In the Tournament profile, click the “Divisions” sidebar item.

Then click “Create Division”.

Give your Division a name, select its initial “Visibility” status, and provide a short description of the Division. Note that the name and Short Info will be visible to others when the Visibility is public (when the Tournament is also public).

Preparing Registration

QView can organize your registration for Teams and for Gear (equipment).

To start, navigate to the Tournament’s Overview page.

Note the “Registration Window” information shown below. It gives the date range for when the Tournament is/will be/had been open for registering Teams and Gear. In this case, the date is 9/8/2026, so it falls in this range. Because of this, its status is “OPEN”, we are given a “Register” button right under the Tournament name and a “Registration” item in the sidebar. The button and sidebar items show/hide based on today’s current date given the Registration Window’s date range.

To control whether registration is currently OPEN or CLOSED, modify the Registration Window dates by clicking the “Edit” button, making the change, and then saving the change.

It’s important to understand the registration process if you are going to have coaches and other program leaders register Teams and Gear for your Tournament. For more on this topic please see the knowledge article on Registering for a Tournament: Teams and Gear.

Once you have reviewed that article, the next steps to take after your Teams and Gear have been registered is to Prepare your Rounds and Games.
